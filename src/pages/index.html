About Our Parish ACK Province
Welcome

A Parish of the Anglican Church of Kenya

St.Phillips Roadblock serves the Kahoya community under the Diocese of Eldoret. We are a caring, listening church — offering Anglican worship, sacraments, guilds, and estate fellowships that bring the Gospel to every neighbourhood.

Whether you are new to faith or returning to church, you are welcome at our services. Come as you are; leave encouraged in Christ.

  • Book of Common Prayer tradition
  • Baptism, confirmation, weddings & funerals
  • Pastoral care and community outreach

New to our parish?

  1. 1 Arrive on Sunday — ushers will guide you to a seat.
  2. 2 Introduce yourself to the vicar or welcome team after service.
  3. 3 Join an estate fellowship or guild that fits your season of life.
