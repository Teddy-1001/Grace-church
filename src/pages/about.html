About St. Phillips Roadblock

A parish of the Anglican Church of Kenya in Roadblock, Eldoret — caring, listening, and rooted in Christ since 2008.

Who We Are

A Parish of the Anglican Church of Kenya

St. Phillips Roadblock serves the Roadblock and Kahoya communities under the Diocese of Eldoret. We are a caring, listening church — offering Anglican worship, sacraments, guilds, and estate fellowships that bring the Gospel to every neighbourhood.

Whether you are new to faith or returning to church, you are welcome at our services. Come as you are; leave encouraged in Christ.

  • Book of Common Prayer tradition
  • Baptism, confirmation, weddings & funerals
  • Pastoral care and community outreach
  • Estate fellowships across Eldoret
